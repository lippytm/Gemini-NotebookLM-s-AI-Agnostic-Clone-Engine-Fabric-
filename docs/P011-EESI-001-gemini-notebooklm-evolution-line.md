## Independence requirement

The Gemini/NotebookLM first pass must be completed without copying ChatGPT or Claude/Hermes conclusions. Approved schemas and objectives may be shared, but conclusions, notes, evaluations, and product drafts remain separately identified until comparison begins.

## Notebook structure

Create separate source-grounded notebooks for:

1. AI evaluation and capability measurement;
2. continuous improvement and model drift;
3. agent identity, disclosure, consent, and memory provenance;
4. human oversight and approval boundaries;
5. swarm orchestration and least-privilege permissions;
6. programming and blockchain education;
7. Educational Entertainment and Fable 5 development;
8. ethical revenue experiments and consumer protection;
9. privacy, security, accessibility, rights, and environmental review.

Every notebook should contain:

- notebook ID and version;
- objective and scope;
- approved source manifest;
- source dates and retrieval dates;
- claim-to-source matrix;
- shared-source independence groups;
- contradictions;
- unresolved questions;
- confidence and freshness status;
- prohibited data classes;
- output artifacts;
- human reviewer.

## Audio and media safeguards

NotebookLM audio or generated presentations are working educational artifacts, not automatically approved commercial audiobooks or videos. Before publication they require:

- script verification;
- source and quotation review;
- fiction disclosure;
- rights review;
- voice and likeness permissions;
- captions, transcript, navigation, and accessibility review;
- correction and version controls;
- HumanApprovalGate.

## Data restrictions

Do not place passwords, API keys, private keys, seed phrases, private identity documents, full medical or financial records, confidential witnesses, restricted legal materials, or unredacted private evidence into notebooks or general model prompts.

## Evaluation rule

A Gemini score or agreement with another model is not proof of improvement. Improvement requires a defined benchmark, evidence, repeatable tests, baseline comparison, side-effect review, cost and latency review, and an authorized human decision.

## Current boundary

This mirror defines the independent Gemini/NotebookLM work contract. It does not claim that notebooks have already been created, that all sources have been ingested, or that a live synchronized connector exists.
